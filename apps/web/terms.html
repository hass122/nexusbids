2. User Eligibility & Mandatory KYC

To participate on NexusBids, users must meet the following eligibility requirements:

  • Age Requirement: You must be at least 18 years of age or possess the legal capacity to enter binding contracts under Pakistani law.
  • Mandatory Seller KYC: Anyone wishing to list goods for auction must submit a valid Computerized National Identity Card (CNIC) front and back, alongside proof of active address, for administrative approval.
  • High-Value Buyer Verification: Bidders placing aggregate bids exceeding PKR 100,000 must have verified their account via OTP and CNIC verification before bids are authorized.
  • Accuracy of Account Data: Providing forged identity documents, stolen CNICs, or false contact information will result in immediate permanent banning and referral to the FIA Cybercrime Wing.

3. Auction Mechanics & Bidding Rules

Bidding on NexusBids represents an irrevocable legal offer to purchase the item at the bid amount:

  • Binding Commitment: Placed bids cannot be retracted or cancelled. If your bid is the highest valid bid upon auction close, you are legally obligated to complete payment.
  • Bid Increments: All bids must satisfy the minimum bid increment established by the seller or platform tier (e.g., PKR 100, PKR 500, PKR 1,000+).
  • Automatic Proxy Bidding: Our system allows users to define a "Maximum Bid". The system will bid automatically on your behalf in increment tiers only up to your declared ceiling.
  • Anti-Sniping Soft Close: Any bid submitted within the final 2 minutes of an auction extends the auction duration by an additional 2 minutes to ensure all bidders have equal fair opportunity.
  • Reserve Price: Sellers may specify an undisclosed Reserve Price. If the final bid does not satisfy the reserve price, the seller is not compelled to complete the sale.

4. Seller Responsibilities & SLAs

Sellers are the stewards of item authenticity and delivery reliability:

  • Accurate Descriptions: Sellers must disclose all known cosmetic imperfections, functional defects, missing accessories, or restoration histories. Misleading listings constitute fraud.
  • Ownership & Authenticity: Sellers warrant that they possess legal title and immediate physical possession of the listed item. Counterfeit or bootleg goods are strictly prohibited.
  • Fulfillment SLA: The seller must dispatch won and paid items within 3 business days via an approved courier (TCS, Leopards, Trax, PostEx) and upload a verifiable tracking code.
  • Prohibited Items: Firearms, controlled narcotics, pirated media, stolen electronics, non-PTA approved unregistered IMEI devices, and items violating Pakistan Customs Act 1969 are strictly banned.

5. Buyer Responsibilities

Buyers hold reciprocal responsibilities to maintain marketplace fairness:

  • Payment Window: Winning buyers must settle payment via the NexusBids Escrow checkout within 48 hours of auction conclusion.
  • Inspection Period: Once an item is marked "Delivered" by the courier, the buyer has a strict 72-hour inspection window to confirm receipt or open a dispute.
  • Automatic Escrow Release: If no dispute is raised and receipt is not confirmed within 72 hours of tracked delivery, escrow funds are automatically disbursed to the seller.
  • Non-Payment Penalties: Failure to pay for won auctions within 48 hours will trigger a strike on the buyer's account. Three strikes result in permanent account deactivation.

6. Escrow Protection & Fees

NexusBids guarantees that buyer money is protected and seller effort is compensated:

  • NexusBids Escrow Account: Buyer payments are deposited directly into our secured escrow holding account. The seller does not receive funds until the buyer confirms safe delivery.
  • Payment Gateways: We support JazzCash, Easypaisa, 1Link, NayaPay, SadaPay, and direct bank transfers. Gateway processing fees may apply where stated at checkout.
  • Platform Commission: NexusBids retains a nominal marketplace success commission (standard 5% to 8% depending on category) deducted from the final payout to the seller upon completion.

7. Shipping, Tracking & Damage

Items must be delivered with transparent real-time tracking:

  • Packaging Standard: Sellers must pack items securely to withstand courier transit. Inadequate packaging leading to transit damage is the seller's financial liability.
  • Transit Insurance: High-value items (over PKR 50,000) are recommended to be shipped with courier transit coverage.
  • Lost Packages: If a courier officially declares a package lost in transit, the buyer receives an immediate 100% refund from escrow, and NexusBids facilitates courier insurance claims with the seller.

8. Dispute Resolution & Refunds

If an item arrives damaged, non-functional, or significantly different from the seller's listing description:

  • Opening a Dispute: The buyer must initiate a formal dispute within the 72-hour inspection window from their Order Details screen, uploading unboxing video/photographic evidence.
  • Escrow Freezing: Opening a dispute instantly freezes escrow funds. Payout is suspended until both parties and a NexusBids arbiter reach a resolution.
  • Return Shipping: If the dispute is ruled in the buyer's favor for inaccurate listing, the seller must bear return shipping charges, and the buyer receives a 100% refund once tracking confirms return.
  • Buyer's Remorse: NexusBids does not grant refunds for subjective change of mind or buyer's remorse on valid, as-described auction items.

9. Prohibited Marketplace Conduct

NexusBids maintains zero tolerance for manipulation, collusive bidding, or marketplace fraud.

The following behaviors result in immediate account termination, forfeiture of escrow balances, and legal prosecution:

  • Shill Bidding: Bidding on your own auction, using secondary fake accounts, or enlisting friends/family to artificially inflate auction prices.
  • Off-Platform Settlements: Contacting auction participants to complete deals outside of NexusBids Escrow. Off-platform transactions lose all platform protection.
  • Bid Sniping Bots: Utilizing automated unauthorized bots or API scripts to manipulate bidding without human oversight.
  • Defamatory or Harassing Conduct: Abuse directed towards staff, arbiters, or other community members via the messaging system.
