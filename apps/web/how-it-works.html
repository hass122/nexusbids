Buyers

Winning an Auction as a Buyer

01
Create an Account
Sign up with your email and complete KYC (CNIC verification) to unlock bidding. KYC takes 24–48 hours.
02
Find Your Auction
Browse thousands of live auctions across categories. Use filters for price range, time remaining, and more.
03
Place a Bid
Enter your maximum bid. Our auto-bidding system bids on your behalf up to your limit, always keeping you in the lead.
04
Win & Pay
If you win, you'll receive a notification. Complete payment within 48 hours via JazzCash, Easypaisa, or bank transfer.
05
Escrow Protection
Your payment is held in secure escrow. It's only released to the seller after you confirm receipt of the item.
06
Receive & Review
Track your shipment in real time. Once delivered, confirm receipt and leave a review for the seller.
Sellers

Selling on NexusBids

01
Complete Seller KYC
Verify your identity and bank account. NADRA-verified sellers earn buyer trust and higher bid volumes.
02
List Your Item
Upload clear photos, set a starting price, reserve price, and auction duration. Our AI helps optimize your listing.
03
Auction Goes Live
Your item is shown to thousands of verified buyers. Monitor bids in real time from your seller dashboard.
04
Ship & Get Paid
Once buyer pays, ship the item with a tracking number. Funds are released to your account upon delivery confirmation.
