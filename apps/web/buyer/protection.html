NexusBids Marketplace Buyer Protection Policy

Effective Date: October 2026 • Operational Region: Pakistan (Domestic Delivery via TCS, Leopards, M&P, Trax, PostEx).

1. Escrow Trust Mechanism

When a buyer pays for an auction or Buy-Now purchase, 100% of the payment (including item price and shipping fee) is held in NexusBids' secure escrow ledger. The seller never receives payment upfront.

2. 48-Hour Inspection Window

Upon carrier-confirmed delivery, an authoritative 48-hour countdown timer begins. Buyers are entitled to inspect the item to verify functionality, authenticity, and cosmetic condition matching the listing description.

3. Filing a Dispute / Claim

If an item is damaged, defective, counterfeit, or fails to arrive, the buyer can submit a formal claim with photos. Filing a dispute instantly freezes the escrow vault, preventing any auto-release of funds to the seller.

4. Full Refund & Return Terms

If the dispute is decided in the buyer's favor or the seller fails to deliver within the promised SLA, NexusBids issues a 100% refund back to the buyer's original payment method or wallet.
