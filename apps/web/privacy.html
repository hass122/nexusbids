1. Legal Framework & Jurisdiction

NexusBids Technologies Pvt. Ltd. ("NexusBids", "we", "us", or "our") operates an online auction and peer-to-peer marketplace primarily servicing Pakistan. We are dedicated to respecting and safeguarding the personal privacy of all registered members, bidders, sellers, and visitors.

This Privacy Policy is formulated in accordance with the relevant laws and statutory guidelines of the Islamic Republic of Pakistan, including:

  • Prevention of Electronic Crimes Act (PECA) 2016 and applicable data security regulations.
  • Electronic Transactions Ordinance (ETO) 2002 regulating digital consent, electronic records, and legally binding agreements.
  • Pakistan Telecommunication Authority (PTA) regulatory frameworks on subscriber identification and internet communications.
  • State Bank of Pakistan (SBP) e-payment and escrow consumer protection standards.

2. Information We Collect

To operate a reliable, safe, and transparent auction platform, we collect information in the following categories:

A. Personal Account Information

When you register for an account, we collect your full legal name, email address, Pakistani phone number (e.g., 03XX-XXXXXXX), residential address, and preferred account credentials.

B. Transaction & Bid History

We log and retain all bids placed, auction participation history, reserve price interactions, winning settlements, shipping addresses, delivery confirmations, and dispute correspondence.

C. Technical & Telemetry Data

Our infrastructure logs IP addresses, browser user-agents, device fingerprints, login timestamps, and geolocation indicators (city-level) to monitor for bid sniping bots, proxy abuse, and unauthorized access.

3. KYC (Know Your Customer) & CNIC Protection

In order to prevent shilling, fraudulent listing, and financial theft, sellers and high-volume bidders must verify their identity. We treat national identity credentials with the highest level of care:

  • CNIC Identification: We collect your 13-digit Computerized National Identity Card (CNIC) number and front/back photographs for identity verification.
  • Hardware-Level Encryption: All uploaded CNIC media files are encrypted at rest using military-grade AES-256-GCM encryption before being written to storage.
  • Zero Commercial Monetization: We will never sell, lease, or distribute your identity documents to commercial third parties, advertisers, or lead generators.
  • Restricted Human Access: Only designated KYC Compliance Officers under confidentiality bonds possess authorization to inspect documents during manual verification audits.

4. How We Use Your Data

We use the data collected strictly for lawful marketplace purposes, including:

  • Verifying bidder authenticity and validating automatic incremental proxy bids.
  • Facilitating secure escrow holding of winning auction payments until item inspection.
  • Coordinating delivery tracking with partnered Pakistani courier services (TCS, Leopards, Trax).
  • Powering AI-driven recommendation feeds to show auctions matching your demonstrated interests.
  • Deploying AI fraud detection models to detect suspicious collusion between related accounts.
  • Complying with lawful court orders or regulatory directions from law enforcement agencies.

5. Cookies & Local Storage

NexusBids uses essential cookies and browser LocalStorage to provide a seamless user experience:

  • Authentication Cookies: Secure, HttpOnly, SameSite=Strict session cookies that prevent unauthorized session hijacking.
  • Preferences: LocalStorage keys (such as nexusbids_theme) storing your dark/light mode preference and active watchlist notifications.
  • CSRF Protection: Cryptographic token cookies that defend your browser from Cross-Site Request Forgery attacks.

We do not use invasive third-party cross-site advertising trackers or sell your behavioral data to external ad networks.

6. Payments & Escrow Information

Financial transactions are handled through licensed Pakistani financial institutions and payment gateways (including JazzCash, Easypaisa, 1Link, NayaPay, and SadaPay):

  • No Raw Card Storage: NexusBids does not hold or store complete credit/debit card numbers, CVV security codes, or bank account PINs on our servers.
  • Payment Gateway Tokenization: All payments are processed through tokenized APIs certified under PCI-DSS Level 1 compliance.
  • Escrow Transparency: Funds for won items are locked securely in an escrow holding account until the buyer verifies item receipt or the 7-day inspection window elapses.

7. Information Security Standards

We maintain multi-layered administrative, operational, and technological security controls:

  • Transport Security: All web traffic is strictly forced over TLS 1.3 / HTTPS with HSTS preloading.
  • Password Protection: Passwords are hashed using Argon2id / bcrypt with cryptographic salt; plain text passwords are never stored.
  • Two-Factor Authentication (2FA): Time-based one-time passwords (TOTP) supported via standard authenticator apps (Google Authenticator, Microsoft Authenticator).
  • Rate Limiting & Firewalls: Automated defense systems counter distributed brute force attacks, credential stuffing, and bot traffic.

8. Data Retention & Deletion

We retain your personal data only as long as necessary to fulfill marketplace duties and meet statutory audit obligations under Pakistani tax and trade laws:

  • Active Accounts: Maintained for the lifetime of your active profile.
  • Financial & Escrow Records: Retained for 5 years in compliance with FBR and SBP audit mandates.
  • Unverified KYC Submissions: Automatically purged after 90 days if rejected or abandoned.
  • Account Deletion: Upon receiving an account termination request, non-statutory identifying records are securely scrubbed within 30 business days.

9. Your Legal Rights

As a valued user, you hold specific data rights under consumer protection regulations:

  • Right of Access: Request a complete export of your personal information, bid history, and transaction statements.
  • Right to Rectification: Update obsolete shipping addresses, contact numbers, or profile information via your Account Settings.
  • Right to Withdraw Consent: Unsubscribe from marketing communications at any time without forfeiting your core auction capabilities.
  • Dispute Escalation: Lodge complaints regarding privacy malpractice directly to our Data Protection Office or relevant regulatory tribunals.
