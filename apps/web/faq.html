Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions about auctions, bidding increments, escrow payouts, or Pakistani payment gateways? We've got you covered.

When an auction is live, registered bidders can place bids above the current highest bid by at least the specified minimum increment. Your bid is a legally binding commitment. If you are the highest bidder when the countdown timer hits zero, you win the auction and proceed to checkout.
Proxy bidding allows you to enter the highest maximum amount you are willing to pay for an item. The NexusBids engine will then automatically place the lowest required increment on your behalf whenever someone outbids you, up to your ceiling. If the bidding stops below your maximum, you win the item at the lower price!
To prevent unfair last-second bid sniping software, any bid submitted within the final 2 minutes of an auction extends the clock by an additional 2 minutes. This ensures genuine participants have adequate time to respond, just like an authentic live auctioneer calling "going once, going twice".
When you win and pay for an item, your money does not go straight to the seller. Instead, it is locked safely in the NexusBids Escrow vault. The seller only receives payout after the item is delivered via tracked courier and you inspect it (or 72 hours elapse without dispute). If the item doesn't arrive or is fake, you receive a full refund.
