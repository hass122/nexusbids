How it started

A phone, a bad deal, and three very stubborn friends.

Every platform starts somewhere. Ours started with a phone that vanished after we'd paid for it — and three friends who got tired of watching it happen to everyone else.

In early 2026, one of our co-founders tried to sell his old iPhone on a Facebook group. A buyer showed up, paid half the money on the spot, promised to transfer the rest after "checking the phone properly," and then vanished. The phone was gone. The money was gone. And there was absolutely nothing he could do about it — no receipt, no proof, no one to call.

That's normal in Pakistan. Almost everyone who has bought or sold online has a story like this. A bike with a hidden crack in the frame. A "brand new" laptop that was refurbished. An Easypaisa transfer to a seller who blocked the buyer two minutes later. The list goes on.

So we started asking a simple question: what would it take for two strangers in Karachi and Peshawar to trust each other with a hundred thousand rupees?

The answer wasn't more reviews or more warnings. The answer was simple mechanics — verified identity on both sides, money held safely by a third party until the buyer confirms delivery, and a clean record of every bid. Basically, everything a bank does for a wire transfer, but for buying a motorcycle.

We launched NexusBids in late 2026 out of a two-bedroom apartment in Lahore, with three laptops, one whiteboard, and a genuinely unreasonable amount of optimism. Today we're a team of twelve building what we hope becomes Pakistan's most boringly reliable way to trade things online.

What we believe

Four things we won't compromise on.

No money moves until you're happy

When you win an auction, your money goes into an escrow account — not to the seller. They only get paid after you've received the item and confirmed it matches what was listed. If it doesn't, we refund you. No arguments, no waiting on a call center.

Real names, real CNICs, real people

You can't sell on NexusBids without a verified CNIC. High-value bidders go through the same check. We know this makes signup slower — that's the point. If someone tries to scam you, we can tell the police exactly who they are.

Bids land in the same second for everyone

No "refresh the page and hope." Every bid shows up live on everyone's screen the moment it's placed. If you're on a slow connection in Sialkot, you see the same bids as someone on fiber in Islamabad. The playing field is flat.

Built for Pakistan, not adapted to it

JazzCash and Easypaisa aren't bolted-on afterthoughts — they're how most of our users actually pay. Our support team speaks Urdu and English. Our delivery partners are TCS and Leopards. This is a Pakistani product for Pakistani problems.
